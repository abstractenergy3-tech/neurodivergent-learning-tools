Neurodivergent learning tools

Free tools for autistic, ADHD, and AuDHD people, and for the people and places around them. Each tool is calm and quiet, with no sound, no motion, and no timers unless you start one. You can change the text size and colors, and take a break at any time.

Everyone is different. These tools are a starting point. They are not medical advice or therapy.
